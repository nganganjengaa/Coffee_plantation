About Our Plantation

Green Hills Coffee Plantation is a family-run coffee farm set among lush, rolling highlands. We grow high-quality coffee with care for the land and the people who work it, and we welcome visitors, tourists and coffee buyers to see how great coffee is made, from a seedling to cup.

Plantation Activities

Pruning
Cutting back old or unproductive branches so the coffee bushes stay healthy and produce more cherries.
Harvesting
Hand-picking ripe red cherries at the right time to ensure the best flavour and quality.
Drying
Spreading processed beans on raised beds or drying tables to reduce moisture before storage.
